STEP 1

Plan your two-minute video

Your video should be at least 1 minute and no longer than 2 minutes. Use the timing map and script builder to shape what you want to say.

0:00 to 0:20Introduce your name and preferred pronouns.
0:20 to 0:45Share your background, communities, interests, or goals.
0:45 to 1:20Share a story, writer, text, song, or tradition that has stayed with you.
1:20 to 1:50Explain what you hope to explore in African American literature.
1:50 to 2:00End with a thought or question classmates should remember.

Canvas Upload Walkthrough

● ● ●   Canvas discussion reply
  1. Open the AFAM Literature discussion and click Reply.
  2. Write a short greeting.
  3. Choose the media/video option—not the plain attachment option.
  4. Upload your saved video so it appears as a playable video.
  5. Wait for processing to finish before posting.
  6. Post, then play the video to confirm it works.
